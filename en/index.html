Our story

Latin flavor in Sterling and Leesburg

We are a local family business in Virginia. At Tutifrutsy, we want every customer to enjoy fresh products prepared with flavor and served with joy.

Here you will find prepared fruit, minutas, natural drinks, sweet bread, snacks and treats that bring back that taste of home. Customers come back for the fruit, the minutas, the honey and especially the service.

At Tutifrutsy, we serve you better than at home.
